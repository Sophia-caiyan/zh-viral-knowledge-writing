# Mimeng Pattern Research

Use this reference when the request asks about title, subtitle, opening, body, viral structure, or "咪蒙式" writing. This reference summarizes a local study of 1111 article records plus 32 representative articles, without requiring access to the original corpus.

## Research Snapshot

- Dataset inspected: 1111 article records with title, reads, likes, date, author, and position.
- PDF matching: 911 records matched local PDFs.
- Readable body pool: 719 PDFs had at least 1500 extractable Chinese characters.
- Stratified research sample: 116 readable articles across category, high/mid/low like levels, and years.
- Read cap: most reads were capped at 100001, so likes were more useful for relative signal.
- Body pattern in the 116-article sample: median article length around 2547 Chinese characters; median paragraph density around 26 Chinese characters per paragraph; median article contains about 14 questions, 16 dialogue/comment cues, and 68 "you/me/us/reader/fan" references.

Treat these findings as strong writing heuristics, not a complete statistical proof of all 1000+ records. When asked for a rigorous corpus study, inspect more articles and report sampling limits.

## Article Categories

### 1. Emotional Judgment

Purpose: Give the reader a moral verdict they already feel but have not articulated.

Signals:

- Strong enemy: bad logic, social pressure, cowardice, hypocrisy, exploitation.
- Title often includes direct attack, rhetorical question, or moral reversal.
- Body uses evidence as "testimony" and ends with a verdict-like line.

Transfer:

- AI: attack outsourcing thinking, fake productivity, prompt superstition.
- Psychology: attack self-shaming disguised as growth.
- Coaching: attack answer-giving disguised as helping.

### 2. Marriage And Family Conflict

Purpose: Turn private hurt into public language.

Signals:

- The topic starts from intimate roles: spouse, parent, child, in-law, ex, family group.
- The article reframes a private conflict as a value conflict.
- The strongest line often names the hidden transaction: love, labor, body, money, choice.

Transfer:

- Coaching: "You call it communication; it is actually a power contract."
- Psychology: "You are not overreacting; the pattern has been repeating for years."

### 3. Workplace And Money

Purpose: Replace sentimental comfort with harsh but useful rules.

Signals:

- Money is treated as pressure, protection, bargaining power, or proof of responsibility.
- The article often starts with a person who feels wronged, then reveals the larger rule.
- The ending usually upgrades pain into agency.

Transfer:

- AI/work: "Efficiency is not output; it is fewer bad decisions."
- Career: "Your tool stack is not your leverage; your judgment is."

### 4. Female Identity And Shame Reversal

Purpose: Take a shame word and turn it into a power frame.

Signals:

- Starts with an insult, jealousy, body anxiety, "不要脸", or social judgment.
- Reframes the flaw as courage, agency, vitality, or refusal to shrink.
- Best when it defends the reader without making them morally lazy.

Transfer:

- Growth: "You are not too sensitive; you are finally noticing."
- Creator: "You are not shameless; you are willing to be seen."

### 5. Emotional Growth

Purpose: Make pain feel seen, then turn it into a line of meaning.

Signals:

- Often story-led and less instructional.
- Uses one person or relationship to carry the thesis.
- Ending is usually a quotable reframing of love, loss, failure, or dignity.

Transfer:

- Psychology/coaching: use one small scene to reveal a pattern; do not start with theory.

### 6. Story Mystery

Purpose: Pull the reader through curiosity, then land on human truth.

Signals:

- Title contains an odd person, strange job, extreme contrast, or unexpected outcome.
- Opening delays explanation.
- The reveal is not just plot; it changes the meaning of the first scene.

Transfer:

- Product/course stories: make a strange case carry the insight.

### 7. Practical How-To

Purpose: Package method as social pain relief, not as a textbook.

Signals:

- Starts with a failed scene: awkward chat, bad article, workplace mistake.
- Uses numbered points only after the reader feels the pain.
- Each tip includes a social consequence or before/after.

Transfer:

- AI prompts, coaching questions, writing methods, conversation skills.

### 8. Entertainment And Native Interaction

Purpose: Use lightness, voting, collection, or fandom to drive participation.

Signals:

- Reader submissions, lists, polls, jokes, screenshots, or fan memory.
- The structure often matters more than the final insight.

Transfer:

- Community posts, newsletter prompts, audience research, lead magnets.

## Title Mechanics

A strong title usually contains at least 3 of these:

- Reader implication: "你", "我", "我们", or a precise identity.
- Conflict: someone is wrong, something is unfair, a belief is broken.
- Reversal: not X but Y.
- Concrete stake: money, love, body, status, wasted time, lost judgment.
- Emotional verb: 怕, 哭, 忍, 爽, 卡住, 耗尽, 看穿, 放过.
- Social currency: the reader can share it to express a stance.

Reusable title frames:

- "你不是 X, 你是在 Y"
- "别再 X 了, 真正的问题是 Y"
- "为什么你越 X, 越 Y?"
- "我把你当 X, 你把我当 Y"
- "那个 X 的人, 后来 Y"
- "真正毁掉你的, 不是 X"
- "X 时代, 最先被淘汰的不是 Y"
- "我花了 N 年才明白: X"
- "所有 X, 都藏着一个 Y"
- "不是你不努力, 是你少了 X"

Avoid:

- Generic abstraction: "关于 X 的几点思考"
- Empty number titles without real method
- Fake certainty
- Overheated shame that targets vulnerable groups
- Titles that the body cannot repay

## Subtitle And Deck

The subtitle should not repeat the title. It should sharpen the thesis.

Use one of these moves:

- Verdict: "真正可怕的不是 A, 而是 B。"
- Reframe: "这不是 X 的问题, 是 Y 的问题。"
- Permission: "你可以停止把 X 当成自己的错。"
- Promise: "这篇给你一个判断/提问/行动框架。"
- Tension: "看起来是 X, 实际上你正在失去 Y。"

Example pattern:

- Title: "为什么你越让 AI 写, 越不会表达自己?"
- Subtitle: "真正危险的不是 AI 太强, 而是你把判断力也外包了。"

## Opening Mechanics

Do not open with definitions. Use one of:

1. Scene: a recognizable, embarrassing, or costly moment.
2. Confession: "我最近发现一件很可怕的事..."
3. Question: a dilemma that traps the reader.
4. Contradiction: "最会 X 的人, 反而最少 Y."
5. Public event: only if it quickly becomes about the reader.

First-screen checklist:

- A person or situation appears.
- A conflict is visible.
- The reader knows why they should care.
- The article's enemy or hidden cost is hinted.

## Body Mechanics

Use short paragraphs. Alternate emotion and utility.

Default ladder:

1. Scene
2. Conflict
3. Reader recognition
4. Counterintuitive claim
5. Evidence or story
6. Mechanism
7. Practical method
8. Value judgment
9. Ending line

When rewriting a user's long draft, do not follow the original paragraph order by default. First extract:

1. Core claim
2. Reader pain
3. Opposing force
4. Concrete examples worth keeping
5. Practical promise
6. Best ending idea

Then rebuild the article from the strongest emotional or practical engine. A faithful rewrite should preserve meaning, not preserve structure.

For judgment pieces:

- Present the bad logic.
- Show its human cost.
- Refute it with concrete examples.
- Offer a cleaner value.
- End with a verdict.

For practical pieces:

- Start with a failed moment.
- Explain why common advice fails.
- Give numbered rules.
- Attach each rule to a scene.
- End with an identity upgrade.

For story pieces:

- Let the story carry curiosity.
- Reveal information in layers.
- Delay the concept until the reader is emotionally invested.
- End by changing the meaning of the opening.

## Ending Mechanics

End with a line that gives the reader language for sharing.

Good endings do one of:

- Name the real lesson.
- Give permission.
- Draw a boundary.
- Offer a small next action.
- Turn the story into a universal sentence.

Avoid endings that merely summarize bullet points.

## The Core Replicable Essence

The essence is not profanity, drama, or copying a persona. The essence is:

1. Find the reader's private emotion.
2. Name the enemy behind that emotion.
3. Convert the topic into a concrete scene.
4. Deliver a defensible reversal.
5. Give practical value.
6. End with a shareable sentence.
