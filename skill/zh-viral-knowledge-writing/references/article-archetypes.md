# Article Archetypes

Use this reference after diagnosing the topic. Choose one primary archetype. Hybridize only when the source material clearly supports it.

## 1. 情绪代言型

Use for shame, grievance, anxiety, being ignored, being regulated, or feeling unable to speak.

Structure:

1. Concrete hurt scene.
2. Name the private emotion.
3. Reveal the bad logic.
4. Give a strong but bounded judgment.
5. Use evidence, supplied examples, or counterexamples.
6. Offer a new boundary or language.
7. End with permission.

Best titles:

- 情绪代言 + 强判断.
- 情绪代言 + 具体代价.

Avoid: pure venting, humiliating the reader, or claiming all pain has one cause.

## 2. 关系真相型

Use for partner, family, friend, colleague, manager, coach/client, or community relationship topics.

Structure:

1. Relationship friction.
2. The surface problem.
3. Hidden exchange, power structure, or unspoken contract.
4. Acknowledge the other side's possible situation.
5. New judgment.
6. Actionable boundary or sentence.

Best titles:

- 关系冲突 + 反常识.
- 身份镜像 + 具体代价.

Avoid: making one side a cartoon villain unless the supplied material supports that judgment.

## 3. 职场利益型

Use for AI, efficiency, career, money, negotiation, organization, management, and leverage.

Structure:

1. A scene where the reader loses time, status, money, or judgment.
2. Why common comfort or common advice fails.
3. The interest or cost mechanism.
4. Counterintuitive rule.
5. Concrete action plan.
6. Identity upgrade: what kind of operator the reader becomes.

Best titles:

- 具体代价 + 强判断.
- 反常识 + 身份镜像.
- 实用收益 + 具体代价.

Avoid: tool worship, market prophecy, and empty "future trend" claims.

## 4. 成长反鸡汤型

Use for psychology, coaching, self-work, reflection, procrastination, boundaries, and action problems.

Structure:

1. A person is trying hard but nothing changes.
2. Show why the old effort is wrong or too cruel.
3. Explain the psychological or behavioral mechanism with bounded language.
4. Offer a limited new choice.
5. Give a small action.
6. End with permission rather than command.

Best titles:

- 反常识 + 情绪代言.
- 身份镜像 + 实用收益.

Avoid: diagnosing, promising healing, or pretending one tip solves trauma.

## 5. 故事人物型

Use only when the user supplies a real person, public case, interview, or verifiable material.

Structure:

1. Unusual result.
2. Delay the explanation.
3. Show the person's choice.
4. Reveal cost and turning point.
5. Reverse the meaning of the opening.
6. Land on a universal judgment.

Best titles:

- 故事悬念 + 强判断.
- 故事悬念 + 具体代价.

Avoid: invented characters, unnamed "I had a friend" stories, and fake client cases.

## 6. 知识方法型

Use for AI workflows, prompts, coaching questions, conversations, writing, learning, and team practices.

Structure:

1. Failed scene.
2. Why the common method fails.
3. New framework.
4. Three to five actions.
5. A concrete example for each action.
6. The cost of learning the method.
7. A small next step.

Best titles:

- 实用收益 + 具体代价.
- 反常识 + 实用收益.

Avoid: textbook lists before the reader feels the pain.

## Archetype Choice

If two archetypes seem possible:

- Choose emotional representation when the reader's unsaid feeling is the main hook.
- Choose relationship truth when hidden power or exchange is central.
- Choose workplace interest when material involves incentives, management, AI, career, or money.
- Choose growth anti-chicken-soup when the main move is replacing self-blame.
- Choose story/person only with supplied story material.
- Choose knowledge method when the main promise is a repeatable action.
