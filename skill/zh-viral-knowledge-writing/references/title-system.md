# Title System

Use this reference for title, subtitle, opening promise, and title-contract decisions.

## Core Rule

The title is a contract with the body. Before drafting, map each title promise to a body section. If the material cannot deliver the promise, change the title.

## Eight Title Hooks

Use at least two hooks and usually no more than four.

| Hook | Function | Example Shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 情绪代言 | Say the private feeling the reader has not said clearly. | "你不是太敏感，是终于看见了消耗" |
| 强判断 | Give a clear verdict on a behavior, rule, or relationship. | "真正拖垮团队的，不是 AI 不好用" |
| 反常识 | Replace the reader's old explanation. | "不是 prompt 太差，是任务没拆清楚" |
| 身份镜像 | Make a precise reader recognize themselves. | "最容易被 AI 拖累的知识工作者" |
| 具体代价 | Name money, time, dignity, relationship, opportunity, or judgment cost. | "你省下的打字时间，正在变成判断成本" |
| 关系冲突 | Show two roles, interests, or values colliding. | "你以为在帮他，其实是在替他逃避" |
| 故事悬念 | Use an odd person, event, result, or contrast. | "那个最会用 AI 的人，反而最少让 AI 直接写" |
| 实用收益 | Promise a method, question, script, or checklist. | "三个问题，判断你是不是把思考外包了" |

## Title Generation Process

1. Extract six fields:
   - Reader: who should feel named.
   - Situation: where this shows up.
   - Hidden emotion: anxiety, shame, resentment, exhaustion, desire, or fear.
   - Opposing force: false comfort, bad rule, social pressure, weak method, or hidden cost.
   - Core judgment: "not X, but Y".
   - Practical payoff: what the reader can think, say, or do after reading.

2. Generate at least 12 titles:
   - 3 emotional representation titles.
   - 3 counterintuitive titles.
   - 3 concrete-cost titles.
   - 3 method or identity titles.

3. Reject titles that fail hard rules.

4. Score remaining titles out of 100.

5. Pick the strongest title and write the subtitle.

## Title Score

| Dimension | Points |
| --- | ---: |
| Reader relevance | 20 |
| Conflict and tension | 20 |
| Novelty of judgment | 15 |
| Concreteness | 15 |
| Body deliverability | 20 |
| Shareable stance | 10 |

## Hard Reject

Reject a title when:

- The body cannot prove or illustrate a key promise.
- The title promises a method but the body gives only emotion.
- The title humiliates vulnerable readers.
- The title has heat but no defensible claim.
- The title relies on empty phrases: "认知升级", "底层逻辑", "时代红利", "破局", "赋能".
- The title implies current facts, research, or product claims that were not supplied or verified.

## Title Contract

Before drafting, create this internal map:

| Title Element | Body Delivery |
| --- | --- |
| Target reader | Opening scene or paragraph 2 |
| Core conflict | Section 1 |
| Reversal or judgment | First screen or first 20% |
| Evidence, story, or source material | Middle section |
| Method, answer, or reader payoff | Later section |
| Emotional exit | Ending |

Do not display the full contract unless useful. A short "标题合同摘要" is helpful when the user wants to judge quality.

## Subtitle

The subtitle should sharpen, not repeat, the title. Use one move:

- Verdict: "真正可怕的不是 A，而是 B。"
- Reframe: "这不是 X 的问题，是 Y 的问题。"
- Permission: "你可以停止把 X 当成自己的错。"
- Promise: "这篇给你一个判断/提问/行动框架。"
- Tension: "看起来是 X，实际你正在失去 Y。"

## Useful Title Shapes

Adapt, do not fill mechanically:

- "你不是 X，你是在 Y"
- "别再 X 了，真正的问题是 Y"
- "为什么你越 X，越 Y？"
- "真正毁掉你的，不是 X"
- "最先被淘汰的，不是 X"
- "那个 X 的人，后来 Y"
- "我花了很久才明白：X"
- "所有 X，都藏着一个 Y"
- "不是你不努力，是你少了 X"
- "会 X 的人，真正厉害在 Y"

## Title Output Format

When giving title candidates:

- Group by hook type.
- Mark top 3 with scores.
- For the recommended title, include:
  - Why it works.
  - What the body must deliver.
  - Any overpromise risk.
